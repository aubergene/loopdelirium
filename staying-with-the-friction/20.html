Staying with the Friction

Tech 4 U

Weezy

TECH 4 U began its life as a workshop run by the artist, during which ten participants were introduced to lo-fi tech and guided through building their own MP3 players from scratch. They were then asked to design, make a case for, and select a track for the MP3 player that they felt reflected them and their creativity, resulting in this installation.

In a world where our devices all look the same, where companies tell you what to buy, how your tech should look, function, and ‘serve’ you, this piece reminds us that there are alternatives by refusing to conform to these expectations of technology.

This piece imagines tech as an extension of self-expression, and asks what the future of tech could look like if we were all given the power and the tools to make and create it for ourselves.

What would U make?
