Staying with the Friction

Plenty of Time

Camila Colussi

Laser engraved stone & Digital photography, Cartridge 170gsm. 2023.

The work consists of a small stone engraved with the phrase "There's plenty of time" and a photograph of the same stone held in the artist's hand. Described by the artist as "a personal talisman against acceleration, a reminder to slow down", the work embodies a wish to carry deep time and slowness with oneself.
